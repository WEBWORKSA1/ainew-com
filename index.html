Business strategy

Competitive Positioning Map

When you need to see where your offer stands against alternatives before choosing a direction.

You are a strategy consultant. I run [your business] selling [your product] to [target customer]. My main competitors are [competitor 1], [competitor 2] and [competitor 3].
Compare us on the five factors buyers care about most in [your industry], and pick those factors yourself. Be candid about where we are weaker.
Constraints: base claims only on what I tell you and on widely known facts, and flag any assumption.
Format: a table of factors versus players with a 1-5 score, then three white-space opportunities and one recommended positioning in two sentences.
