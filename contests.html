How it runs

  1. Register

    Get the full brief, judging rubric and key dates by email.

  2. Build

    Use any tools. Solve a real, repeatable task for a real or realistic business.

  3. Submit

    A short write-up, a 3-minute demo video and a reusable template or prompt set.

  4. Get judged

    A panel scores entries against the published rubric. Winners are featured on AiNew.

Judging criteria

40%

Impact

Time or money saved, backed by a believable measure.

25%

Originality

A fresh approach, not a copy of a tutorial.

20%

Clarity

Could a non-expert follow and trust it?

15%

Reusability

How easily others can adapt it.

Official rules summary

  1. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. Paying or supporting AiNew does not improve the chance of winning.
  2. This is a contest of skill judged against the published criteria; it is not a lottery or game of chance.
  3. Open to individuals aged 18 or over. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees of AiNew and of prize sponsors may not enter.
  4. Entries must be the entrant's original work and must not infringe anyone's rights or include personal data without consent.
  5. Entrants keep ownership of their work and grant AiNew a non-exclusive licence to display and promote entries.
  6. Prize amounts, entry dates and judging dates are confirmed on this page before entries open; the full rules sent to registrants govern.
  7. Winners may need to confirm eligibility and identity before receiving a prize. Taxes on prizes are the winner's responsibility.
  8. AiNew may disqualify entries that break the rules, and may modify or cancel a contest if required by law or circumstances beyond its control.
