How AI-ready is your business?

Ten quick questions, about two minutes. Get an instant readiness score and a recommended next step. No email needed to see your result.

1. Where does most of your business information live?
2. How much of your team's week goes on repetitive tasks (copying, sorting, answering the same questions)?
3. How do people use AI tools at work today?
4. Do you have rules on what data can be put into AI tools?
5. Can you measure the tasks you'd want to improve (volume, time, cost)?
6. Who would own an AI project?
7. How comfortable is your team with trying new software?
8. How do your main apps connect (CRM, email, accounting, helpdesk)?
9. Is budget available for a small pilot?
10. How would you check that AI output is right before it reaches customers?
