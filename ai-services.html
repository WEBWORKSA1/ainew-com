Common questions

Is the plan really free?

Yes. The written starting plan costs nothing and carries no obligation. If you later hire a specialist through us, you agree scope and price with them before any work starts.

Who are the specialists?

Independent consultants, developers and automation builders who have applied to our talent network and shown relevant past work. We match on industry, task and budget.

What happens to my data?

Your answers are used only to prepare your plan and, with your permission, to introduce a specialist. We never sell lead data. Read the privacy policy.

We're very early. Is this still useful?

Yes. Many plans start with a usage policy and two low-risk pilots. The small business guide is a good primer while you wait.
